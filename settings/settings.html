Storage

Screenshots are automatically saved to Downloads/Screenshot Bookmark/ on your computer. This folder persists even if you clear browser data.

Auto-save location

Windows: C:\Users\[you]\Downloads\Screenshot Bookmark\

Mac: /Users/[you]/Downloads/Screenshot Bookmark/

Import Mother Folder

Danger Zone

Note: Files already saved in your Downloads folder are NOT affected.

Clear library index

Removes all entries from the extension's internal library. Files in Downloads/Screenshot Bookmark/ are preserved.

Delete everything

Clears the library index AND the internal image cache. Files in Downloads are still preserved.
